Flights from your Google Calendar

So "check me in for my flight" works even when the flight is only in your calendar. Checked on 29 October 2026.

Short answer: nothing needs changing on Google's side. No Google Cloud project, no consent-screen edits, no Google review. You click one "Connect Google Calendar" button and press Allow on Google's page, once.

What I found

  • Ability doesn't have its own Google app. Gmail is connected through Travila's connected apps (run by Pipedream), which use Pipedream's own Google app. Google has already reviewed that app, so we never needed a Google Cloud project, a consent screen or Google verification.
  • Pipedream has a separate Google Calendar app that works the same way. I checked Travila's list of available apps today: Google Calendar is on it.
  • The Gmail connection can't simply be widened to include your calendar. Pipedream's Gmail app only asks Google for Gmail permissions, and each Pipedream app has its own Google permission page. So Calendar is a second, separate connect step.
  • On Google's page you'll be asked to allow viewing and editing calendar events. That's the fixed set of permissions Pipedream's Calendar app asks for. Ability will only read events and never change or add any.
  • Getting a "view only" permission page would mean setting up Ability's own Google app and going through Google's review (privacy policy, demo video, usually weeks). I don't think it's worth that now.

What you'll see

  • In Settings › Connections, next to Gmail, a Connect Google Calendar button. The assistant will offer it too when it can't find a flight. You click it, choose your Google account and press Allow. That's it.
  • Calendar events that look like a flight (an airline flight number such as the ones Gmail adds, or one you typed in, with a route and time) become trips with a trip card, the same as a booking from Gmail.
  • If the same flight is already there from Gmail or chat, it's matched by route and departure time, so you never see it twice.
  • Calendar events often don't have the booking reference. Then the trip card asks for it, once, and the assistant points you there.

How often and what it costs

  • No new background job. The calendar is checked at the same moments Gmail already is: once an hour, and when you use the app and the last check is over 15 minutes old.
  • Each check is one small request to Google through Travila, the same kind of request the Gmail check already makes several of.
  • Only events that look like flights go to the AI to read, and each one only once (again if you change it). That's a fraction of a cent per flight.

What I still can't check until you connect

I can't read a calendar until one is connected, so the first real read happens after you click Connect. I'll check it works on your own calendar then. Nothing goes live on the website until you say so.
